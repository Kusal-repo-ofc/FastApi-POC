## 🧠 Key Technical Discussion

### 1. Python 3.14 & GIL (Global Interpreter Lock)

* The team discussed how **Python 3.14 behaves differently with GIL**
* You cannot disable GIL in a standard installation, but:
  * A **special free-threaded build** is required for that
* A workaround was tested by setting environment variables

👉 From meeting chat:

* CMD approach: `set PYTHON_GIL=0`
* PowerShell approach: `$env:PYTHON_GIL = "1"`

### 2. PowerShell vs CMD Issue (Important finding)

* It was discovered:
  * You were using **PowerShell**, not CMD
